The paper of record

Built at full standard: rules frozen before capture, fail-closed audits, replication before claims.

In-Context Availability Reorganizes MoE Expert Routing

Cross-generation study (Qwen3.5 + 3.6): when a model's output already sits verbatim in context, layer-14 routing reorganizes under rules frozen before capture. The attention carrier is located and severed; the coalition replicates across a model generation under frozen predictions; intervention nulls bound the claim. Direct implication: multi-turn evaluations partly measure availability, not capability.

Confirmatory · preregistered · replicated DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21892999

The Generation Half

Expert specialization is invisible in prefill and emerges during generation — one generalist wins 18/20 domains at prefill; twenty distinct per-domain experts win during generation. Length-matched control; replicates at 122B.

No Single Safety Gate
